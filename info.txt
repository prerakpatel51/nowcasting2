  9. SUMMARY OF KEY IMPACT

  1. No latent compression — Your diffusion model works in pixel space (1 channel,
  64×64) rather than latent space (32 channels, 32×32). This means:
    - No separate VAE training stage needed
    - But the diffusion model must learn directly in pixel space
    - The UNet processes 1-channel input instead of 32-channel input
  2. Wider but shallower UNet — 256 base channels (vs 128) but 3 levels (vs 4). This
  partially compensates for operating in pixel space by giving more capacity per
  level.
  3. Much longer temporal context — 16×30min = 8hr input, 12×30min = 6hr output (vs
  ~20min input, ~40min output). The temporal transformer must handle many more time
  steps.
  4. Smaller spatial extent — 64×64 (vs 128×128 or 256×256), so the 3D convolutions
  and FFTs are computationally cheaper per frame.
  5. Training robustness — More patience (20 vs 6), warmup (500 steps), DDP instead of

  9. SUMMARY OF KEY IMPACT

  1. No latent compression — Your diffusion model works in pixel space (1 channel,
  64×64) rather than latent space (32 channels, 32×32). This means:
    - No separate VAE training stage needed
    - But the diffusion model must learn directly in pixel space
    - The UNet processes 1-channel input instead of 32-channel input
  2. Wider but shallower UNet — 256 base channels (vs 128) but 3 levels (vs 4). This
  partially compensates for operating in pixel space by giving more capacity per
  level.
  3. Much longer temporal context — 16×30min = 8hr input, 12×30min = 6hr output (vs
  ~20min input, ~40min output). The temporal transformer must handle many more time
  steps.
  4. Smaller spatial extent — 64×64 (vs 128×128 or 256×256), so the 3D convolutions
  and FFTs are computationally cheaper per frame.
  5. Training robustness — More patience (20 vs 6), warmup (500 steps), DDP instead of
   DP, and explicit FP32 precision.
